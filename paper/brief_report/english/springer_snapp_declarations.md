# Springer SNAPP Declarations Template

Use this checklist when submitting to Springer Nature SNAPP, including Universal Access in the Information Society.

Last updated from the UAIS SNAPP declaration screen: 2026-07-04.

## Current KOSHA-Braille Brief Report

### Publishing Policy

Select:

> I have read and understood the publishing model policy, and I am submitting according to this policy.

Note: UAIS is hybrid. This checkbox does not by itself select gold open access. If Springer later asks for the publishing route, choose based on funding and publication strategy.

### Competing Interests

Select:

> No, I declare that the authors have no competing interests as defined by Springer, or other interests that might be perceived to influence the results and/or discussion reported in this paper.

Manuscript-compatible statement:

> The author declares no competing financial or non-financial interests.

### Dual Publication

Select:

> No, the results/data/figures in this manuscript have not been published elsewhere, nor are they under consideration by another publisher.

### Authorship

Select:

> I confirm the corresponding author has read the journal policies and submit this manuscript in accordance with those policies.

### Author Contributions Statement

Paste:

> Y.K. conceived and designed the study, implemented the KOSHA-Braille pipeline and Korean braille encoder, acquired and processed the KOSHA MSDS data, performed validation and analysis, prepared the figures and tables, wrote the manuscript, and approved the final version.

Korean reference:

> Y.K.는 연구를 구상하고 설계했으며, KOSHA-Braille 파이프라인과 한국어 점자 인코더를 구현하고, KOSHA MSDS 데이터를 수집·처리했으며, 검증과 분석을 수행하고, 그림과 표를 준비하고, 원고를 작성하고 최종본을 승인하였다.

### Third Party Material

Select:

> No, all of the material is owned by the authors and/or no permissions are required.

Rationale: the manuscript does not reuse third-party copyrighted figures, images, or supplementary material requiring permission. Public KOSHA/Public Data Portal records are cited as source data and handled under data availability.

### Data Availability

Select:

> Yes. I used or generated research data in this study.

Paste:

> The dataset generated and analyzed during this study is publicly available on HuggingFace Datasets at https://huggingface.co/datasets/Yuyongkim/inconvenience-msds. The source code, encoder, decoder, pipeline, web service, evaluation harness, and verification scripts are available at https://github.com/yuyongkim/inconvenience-msds. The source MSDS records are publicly available through the Korea Public Data Portal at https://www.data.go.kr/data/15001197/openapi.do. Dataset license: CC BY 4.0; code license: MIT.

Do not paste manuscript citation numbers such as `[13]`, `[14]`, or `[15]` into SNAPP fields. Use URLs.

### Research Funding

Select:

> No, this research did not receive funding.

## Reuse Notes

- SNAPP says several declaration fields replace any statement inside the manuscript. Keep the manuscript consistent, but treat the SNAPP text as the published source of truth.
- For single-author papers, use initials in form fields: `Y.K.`
- Re-check this guide if Springer changes the SNAPP wording or the manuscript data/code URLs change.
