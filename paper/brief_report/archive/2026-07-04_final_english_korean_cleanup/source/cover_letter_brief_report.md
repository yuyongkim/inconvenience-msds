Dear Professor Antona,

I am resubmitting the manuscript "KOSHA-Braille: Infrastructure-Grade Accessibility for Korean Chemical Safety Information" for consideration as a Brief Report, following your suggestion to resubmit the prior UAIS Research-category submission (ID: aae028f8-e8f0-43c1-888c-c986dfe01198) under the Brief Reports category. The resubmission package identifies the article type as "Brief Report" on the title page and manuscript.

The manuscript reports the construction, validation, and public release of a Korean chemical safety MSDS braille corpus (48,966 chemicals; 401,272 non-empty sections released from a 769,897-row source-section database; approximately 232.3 million braille cells), produced by a deterministic encoder compliant with the 2017 Korean Braille Standards. The encoder is cross-validated at 100% agreement over 442 test cases against an independently developed open-source Korean braille converter; the released Korean round-trip golden set is recovered at 45/45. Dataset, encoder, decoder, evaluation harness, reference web service, and verification scripts are released openly (dataset under CC BY 4.0, code under MIT).

The work aligns with the journal's interest in accessibility infrastructure, standardization, policy, and multilingual accessibility. It is positioned as a design-and-release study, not as an end-user evaluation study; a reading-comprehension user study with visually impaired readers is identified in the manuscript as essential follow-up and deliberately scoped out of the present submission.

The manuscript is original work, has not been published or submitted elsewhere, and is not derived from a thesis or technical report under separate review. The author has no competing interests, and the work received no external funding. Declarations and a Data and Code Availability statement are included in the manuscript.
