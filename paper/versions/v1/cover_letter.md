Dear Editor,

I am submitting the manuscript **"KOSHA-Braille: Infrastructure-Grade Accessibility for Korean Chemical Safety Information"** for consideration as an Original Article.

The paper describes the construction, validation, and public release of a Korean chemical safety MSDS braille corpus (48,966 chemicals; 401,272 non-empty sections released from a 769,897-row source-section database; approximately 232.3 million braille cells), produced by a deterministic encoder compliant with the 2017 Korean Braille Standards. The encoder is cross-validated at 100% agreement over 442 test cases against an independently developed open-source Korean braille converter; the released Korean round-trip golden set is recovered at 45/45. Dataset, encoder, decoder, evaluation harness, reference web service, and the verification scripts that regenerate every numerical claim in the paper are released openly (dataset under CC BY 4.0, code under MIT).

The work aligns with the journal's interest in accessibility infrastructure, standardization, policy, and multilingual accessibility. It is positioned explicitly as a design-and-release study, not as an end-user evaluation study: a reading-comprehension user study with visually impaired readers is identified in Section IX as essential follow-up and is deliberately scoped out of the present submission. We considered this the more responsible boundary for an infrastructure-grade release than reporting an under-powered user study; the manuscript states the scope of validation and the scope of non-validation explicitly so reviewers can locate the boundary without inference.

The manuscript is original work, has not been published or submitted elsewhere, and is not derived from a thesis or technical report under separate review. The author has no competing interests, and the work received no external funding. Declarations and a Data and Code Availability statement are included in the manuscript.

I would be glad to suggest reviewers on request.
